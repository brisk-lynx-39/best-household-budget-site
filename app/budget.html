How the Riveras set a budget

Three rules that keep the limits honest.

  • Fixed costs first. Housing, utilities and insurance are set from the actual bills, not from a guess — the mortgage is $1,850 every month.
  • Groceries from the average. The $650 groceries limit is the average of the last three months’ shops, rounded up a little for a holiday month.
  • One fun limit, shared. Dining Out, Fun and Shopping each get their own cap so a good week in one cannot quietly eat the others.
