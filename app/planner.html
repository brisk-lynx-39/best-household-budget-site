Making the rule fit a real household

  • High housing costs? In an expensive city, needs of 60% and savings of 15% still beat no plan at all — the split matters less than having one.
  • Count the transfer as savings. The Riveras move $1,200 to savings on the 25th of every month, before they can spend it. Automatic beats willpower.
  • Wants are not waste. Pizza night and the cinema are in the plan on purpose. A budget with no fun in it is a budget nobody keeps.
