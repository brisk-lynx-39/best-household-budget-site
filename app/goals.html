How the goals get funded

  • Pay yourself first. $1,200 leaves the checking account for savings on the 25th of every month, the same day the budget closes.
  • Windfalls get split. When tutoring pays more than usual, half goes to the emergency fund and half to the vacation goal.
  • Small goals first. The holiday-gifts goal is nearly done, and finishing it frees its share for the car fund next.
